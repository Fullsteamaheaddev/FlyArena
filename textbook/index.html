Compiling the Fly Brain

From Connectome to Computation

A complete nervous system is now a data structure: 165,122 neurons, 104 million synapses. This book documents a pipeline that reads the wiring diagram, proposes the computations it performs, builds the families of models the wiring permits, and identifies the experiments that decide between them — worked end-to-end on three circuits of the male Drosophila CNS.
