# Appendix B: References and Sources

## Data and models

- Male CNS connectome v1.0, Janelia FlyEM and Google Research, CC-BY 4.0.
  https://male-cns.janelia.org
- Cook et al. 2019, whole-animal connectomes of both *C. elegans* sexes, Nature. The
  hermaphrodite chemical and gap-junction graphs used in Chapter 2, as distributed by
  Netzschleuder, corrected July 2020.
- OpenWorm and WormAtlas cell tables, for *C. elegans* neurotransmitter assignments.
- flybody, Vaxenburg et al. 2024, the MuJoCo fly body. https://github.com/TuragaLab/flybody
- flyvis, Lappalainen et al. 2024, the trained optic-lobe model. https://github.com/TuragaLab/flyvis
- FlySuite, walking trajectories and the 218 Hz wing-beat generator. https://github.com/TuragaLab/FlySuite

## Neuron model and calibration

- Shiu et al. 2024, a leaky integrate-and-fire model of the FlyWire connectome, Nature.
  Source of the base LIF parameters and of the sugar, bitter and veto benchmarks.
- Pugliese et al. 2025, connectome simulations of a walking central pattern generator.
  Source of the neuron-size scaling, the 5-synapse threshold, the BDN2 benchmark, and
  the 3% figure for descending neurons that produce rhythm.
- Turner et al. 2008, olfactory representations by Kenyon cells, J Neurophysiol. The
  7.4% sparseness target.
- Olsen and Wilson 2008, lateral presynaptic inhibition in the antennal lobe, Nature.
- Gruntman and Turner 2013, integration of Kenyon-cell inputs.

## Central complex

- Turner-Evans et al. 2020, the neuroanatomical ultrastructure and function of a
  biological ring attractor, Neuron. The withheld Delta7-block result of Chapter 9.
- Hulse et al. 2021, a connectome of the central complex, eLife. Background for the
  column labels and the PFN/hΔB pathways.

## Descending control and behaviour

- Braun et al. 2024, descending networks, Nature.
- Cande et al. 2018, optogenetic dissection of descending control, eLife. Forward-
  walking and grooming DN assignments.
- Bidaye et al. 2014 and 2020, the moonwalker descending neuron and backward walking.
- Rayshubskiy et al. 2020, DNa02 and steering.
- Sapkal et al. 2024, descending neurons for walking.
- von Reyn et al. 2014 and Namiki et al. 2018, the giant fibre and takeoff descending
  neurons.
- Inagaki et al. 2012 and LeDue et al. 2016, hunger-dependent sugar and bitter
  sensitivity.
- Maye et al. 2007, order in spontaneous behavior, PLoS ONE 2:e443. Brembs 2011,
  Proc R Soc B 278:930. Sources for the bout statistics.
- Geurten et al. 2014, saccadic body turns in walking *Drosophila*, Front Behav Neurosci.
- Seeds et al. 2014, grooming sequences, eLife.
- Dethier 1957 and Kim and Dickinson 2017, local search after feeding, Curr Biol.
- Kim et al. 2015, efference copy in *Drosophila* visuomotor processing, Nat Neurosci.
  The justification for escape gating.
- Tammero and Dickinson 2002, collision-avoidance saccades in free flight, J Exp Biol.
- Dickinson 1999 and Dickinson et al. 1999, haltere reflexes and the quasi-steady
  aerodynamic force model, Phil Trans R Soc B and Science 284:1954.
- Ribeiro et al. 2018, LC10 visual projection neurons in courtship, Neuron. Deutsch et
  al. 2020, pIP10 and courtship pursuit, Curr Biol.

## Neuromodulation

- Yu et al. 2016, insulin and AKH signalling in octopaminergic neurons regulate
  starvation-induced hyperactivity, eLife 5:e15693.
- Lee and Park 2004, AKH and starvation-induced hyperactivity, Genetics 167:311.
- Yang et al. 2015, octopamine and starvation-induced hyperactivity, PNAS. The Tβh
  phenotype matched in Chapter 14.
- Suver et al. 2012, octopamine neurons mediate flight-induced modulation of visual
  processing, Neuron.
- Marella et al. 2012, dopaminergic modulation of sucrose acceptance, Neuron.

## Theory

- Amari 1977, dynamics of pattern formation in lateral-inhibition type neural fields,
  Biol Cybern. The field model of Chapter 8.
- Dasgupta, Stevens and Navlakha 2017, a neural algorithm for a fundamental computing
  problem, Science. The locality-sensitive-hashing reading of the mushroom body.
